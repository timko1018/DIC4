\section{Related Work and Context}
The Food Waste Index establishes the scale of household waste through measurement and reporting \cite{unep}. Our study uses recipe-level allocation records to measure whether a proposed plan makes use of earlier-dated stock. This measure can be calculated without collecting household records and does not estimate waste avoided. The Model Context Protocol (MCP) defines a standard way for applications to discover and call tools \cite{mcp}. In our design, the tool boundary lets the same allocator serve different clients and makes allocation decisions independently replayable. The 2025-11-25 protocol revision anchors the tool contract described in this draft.
