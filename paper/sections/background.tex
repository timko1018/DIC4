\section{Introduction}
The United Nations Environment Programme estimated that households accounted for 60\% of food waste at the retail and consumer levels in 2022 \cite{unep}. A meal-planning assistant can help a household find recipes that use food already on hand. The decision requires more detail than an ingredient list. Two packages of the same ingredient may have different dates, units, or quantities. A recommendation should identify which packages support the meal and which requirements still need to be filled.

This draft proposes a reproducible allocation rule, a trace format linking each recommendation to the inventory snapshot, and a controlled replay protocol. The household inventory setting and modular manuscript organization draw on a prior course project \cite{course}.
