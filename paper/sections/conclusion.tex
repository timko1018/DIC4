\section{Conclusion}
A versioned, batch-level allocation trace makes it possible to audit the inventory basis of a meal recommendation. The proposed allocator treats uncertainty as an explicit outcome and separates feasibility from date-based ranking. The planned synthetic replay can test trace consistency and stock coverage. Household adoption and waste reduction require a separate field evaluation. The next step is to implement the tools, release the replay fixtures, and measure feasibility and date-aware use under the specified comparisons.
