\begin{abstract}
Meal suggestions can overlook differences between household inventory batches, even when they name the correct ingredient. We propose a meal-planning assistant that allocates recorded inventory to recipe requirements before ranking candidate meals. A deterministic allocator consumes earlier-dated eligible batches first and reports both the selected batches and any shortfall. A read-only Model Context Protocol interface exposes the inventory snapshot and allocation result to the assistant. We specify a planned replay study using synthetic household inventories to compare feasibility, near-date stock use, and trace accuracy under three allocation policies. The proposed method and evaluation protocol form the contribution of this draft.
\end{abstract}
