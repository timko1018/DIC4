# paper-references Specification

## Purpose
Make the report, protocol revision, and reference course project independently traceable.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Resolve bibliography entries
The manuscript SHALL include BibTeX via the manuscript entry point and cite the report, protocol, and course reference.

#### Scenario: Review references
- **WHEN** LaTeX and BibTeX finish their final runs
- **THEN** all three cited sources resolve in the bibliography without undefined citation markers.
